REACH! 

A webcam-powered reaction game you play by reaching for targets on screen. Your browser tracks your wrists, so no controller or special equipment is needed.
How to play
1. Open the game in your browser and select Enable camera. Allow camera access when prompted.
2. Stand back until your upper body and both wrists are visible.
3. Select Start game and play for 30 seconds.
4. Reach into green circles with either wrist to score. The faster you react, the more points you earn (100–1,000 points per hit).
5. Avoid red circles. Touching one deducts 300 points. Your score never goes below zero.
Green targets disappear after a short time. An untouched green target counts as a miss, but doesn't deduct points. Untouched red targets don't affect your score.
Difficulty
Time elapsed	Targets that can appear together
0–10 seconds	1 green + 1 red
10–20 seconds	2 green + 1 red
20–30 seconds	3 green + 2 red


Controls & results
- Full Screen: Expand the game view. Use Exit Full Screen or Esc to leave it. Start and Stop are also available in full screen.
- Show pose: Toggle the body-tracking overlay.
- Scoreboard: Displays remaining time, score, green hits, red hits, misses, and average green-target reaction time.
- Download CSV: Save a record of target events, including reaction times, hit/miss outcomes, and score changes.
Troubleshooting
- Camera won't start: Allow camera access in your browser's site settings, then reload the page. Close other apps using the webcam if needed.
- Model won't load: Check your internet connection and reload. The game downloads its pose-tracking files when it starts.
- Targets aren't registering: Move back so your wrists stay visible. Use good, even lighting and keep your hands in the camera frame.
- Tracking feels slow: Close demanding tabs or apps, and try a different browser such as Chrome.
- Full screen won't open: Use the Full Screen button on the game view. Browser or device restrictions may limit full-screen support.
Privacy
Pose tracking runs on your device in the browser. The game does not require an account, a paid API, or uploading your webcam footage. Model and runtime files are downloaded from public CDNs.
