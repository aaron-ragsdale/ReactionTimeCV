How to play

Reach into green circles with either wrist. Faster green hits earn more points (100–1,000 each). Red circles cost 300 points if touched. After 10 seconds, two green targets can appear at once; after 20 seconds, up to three. Red circles become more frequent too. Missed green circles count as misses but do not deduct points. The preview is mirrored. Select Full Screen on the camera view for immersive play (Escape exits full screen).
