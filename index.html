How to play

Reach into green circles with your palm or fingertips. Faster green hits earn more points (100–1,000 each). Red circles cost 300 points if touched. Targets arrive at unpredictable intervals, with at most two green circles and one red circle visible at once. Green targets now disappear sooner, and hand tracking uses light smoothing plus extra green-hit tolerance. Missed green circles count as misses but do not deduct points. The preview is mirrored. On iPad, select Smooth tracking before enabling the camera. Select Full Screen on the camera view for immersive play (Escape exits full screen).
