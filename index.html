Ready to reach?

Enable your camera, stand back so your upper body is visible, then start the 30-second challenge.

How to play

Keep your upper body in view. A new green target appears after each hit or timeout. Move your wrist into the circle to score. Your camera preview is mirrored to make movement intuitive.

Tracking

Camera is off.

Privacy: pose inference runs locally in your browser. No camera frames are uploaded by this app. On first load, your browser downloads the MediaPipe JavaScript/WASM and model from public hosting; no account or paid API is needed.
